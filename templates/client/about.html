Who We Are

We are Isotradex, a Isotradex Group company. Established in 2007, we are the largest broker-agnostic social wealth management and the oldest copy trading platform in the world. We revolutionized copy trading by creating a “one of its kind” socially driven broker and platform-agnostic social network.

We enable you to invest your capital and manage your wealth more efficiently. With the help of our large and diverse community, technology, and open ecosystem, we help you make informed investment decisions in a digitally safe and trusted environment.
